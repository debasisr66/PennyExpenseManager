PENNY V2 — START HERE

Open the hosted app at https://debasisr66.github.io/PennyExpenseManager/.
To use a downloaded copy, extract the entire ZIP and open index.html. Keep its CSS and JavaScript files beside it.

All expenses and budgets are in INR (₹).

QUICK TRY-OUT
1. Search #groceries in the top bar.
2. Add a ₹250 expense named Test lunch #groceries, with #family in Tags.
3. Use + Tag beside it to add #reimbursable. Use × to remove a tag.
4. Edit the amount, then refresh to check that it is saved.
5. Lower the monthly budget to trigger the red warning.
6. In Add expense choose Use voice if you want to dictate. Start listening only when ready. Review the draft, then save yourself.
7. Use the conversion bar to estimate another currency, or convert a foreign amount to INR. It does not change your expenses.
8. Read What's New for the V2 changes.

TEST DOCUMENTS
TEST-CASES.md contains full steps and expected results.
TEST-RESULTS.md records checks that passed and checks still requiring your device.

Voice support depends on your browser, microphone permission, and speech service. Keyboard entry always remains available. Real microphone capture was not tested by the agent.

Data stays in the same browser on the same device. V1 records retain their numerical values; the previous USD label is corrected to INR without applying an exchange rate.
