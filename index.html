Natural phrases like “this month”, “last month”, “over 100” and “under 500” work too.

      What’s New

      V3.3.7

      24 November 2026

      • Voice never saves a guess. A voice entry no longer picks “Other” for you or keeps a filler description such as “expense”. If Penny did not hear the category or the item, the field stays empty and you choose it. If no date was heard, the date is today. Zero or negative amounts were already refused and still are.
      • Hindi and Spanish voice is assisted. Your phone may mishear, and Penny understands only common words. Check the amount, category and date. Nothing is saved until you tap Save. After a failed capture Penny opens the keyboard microphone, and after two in a row it says so plainly and opens the Tips.
      • Easier to tap on a phone. Buttons such as Edit, short tags and the exchange-rate rows are now at least 44 px wide and tall, and check boxes are bigger. The smallest text is now 11 px, and large phone text sizes fit the screen without sideways scrolling.
      • New categories: Utilities and Staff salary. Voice entry in English, Hindi and Spanish understands words such as electricity, water, gas, recharge, salary and maid. Past entries are not moved. To move a bill, edit its last entry’s category. A backup that uses a new category does not import into 3.3.3 or older.
      • Dark and light theme. The ◐ button in the header switches between System, Light and Dark. System follows your phone or computer. Your choice stays on this device and is not part of your backups. Colours were also made easier to read, and the header now fits a phone in two short rows.
      • Recurring bills. Tick “Recurring bill” when you add or edit an expense, and choose how often it repeats. The new Bills panel shows each bill as Paid, Due soon, Overdue or “Stopped?”. Nothing new is stored: it uses a tag you can search with #recurring.
      • Looks recurring. Penny still notices bills you pay most months. Tick “Recurring bill” next time you add one to track when it is due.
      • Excel import and export removed. Use CSV, or a full JSON backup, which also carries budgets, tags and preferences. Penny now has no third-party code.
      • Settings and start fresh. Manage your default budget and saved tags. Delete one month or all months; full reset needs two confirmations and keeps tags.
      • Private diagnostics. Errors only by default, with optional 30-minute debug logging and a reviewed export.
      • Safer Hindi drafts. Grouped amounts, Hindi number words and Hinglish are checked before wording. Unresolved fields stay empty for correction.
      • Offline Hindi and Spanish expense parsing (assisted). Hindi and Spanish voice is assisted: Penny extracts common amounts, dates, categories and payment methods on the device, then creates a controlled English draft. Check it before saving.
      • Clearer voice diagnostics. Capture failures, unfamiliar vocabulary and ready drafts now have different messages.
      • Voice works across repeated attempts. Mobile sessions reset cleanly, keep partial speech and recover when a browser misses its final event.
      • Take your data with you. Import and export CSV, plus complete JSON backups.
      • Know how you paid. UPI, cards, cash and other methods have searchable tags.
