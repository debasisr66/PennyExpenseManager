What’s New

V3.2.0

19 October 2026

  • Settings and start fresh. Manage your default budget and saved tags. Delete one month or all months; full reset needs two confirmations and keeps tags.
  • Private diagnostics. Errors only by default, with optional 30-minute debug logging and a reviewed export.
  • Safer Hindi drafts. Grouped amounts, Hindi number words and Hinglish are checked before wording. Unresolved fields stay empty for correction.
  • Offline Hindi and Spanish expense parsing. Penny extracts amounts, dates, categories and payment methods on the device, then creates a controlled English draft.
  • Clearer voice diagnostics. Capture failures, unfamiliar vocabulary and ready drafts now have different messages.
  • Voice works across repeated attempts. Mobile sessions reset cleanly, keep partial speech and recover when a browser misses its final event.
  • Take your data with you. Import and export CSV/Excel, plus complete JSON backups.
  • Know how you paid. UPI, cards, cash and other methods have searchable tags.
